# Home Lab Opening

Create state only through `new_state(player, company, language)`, which copies `scenarios/home_lab.json`. That JSON is the authoritative initial-state template; skill levels and hardware totals are derived under Rules. Commit once as `new_game`.

## Opening narration

January 2013. You've quit your job. The spare room of your tiny rented apartment is now a home lab: one desk, a secondhand desktop, printed papers on a corkboard, and a whiteboard declaring: "One day, machines will truly understand language."

You have $10,000 in savings, an internet connection, and a hunch that deep learning's wave in image recognition is about to hit language. Your knowledge of Basic Text Processing — regex, word counts and cleaning text — is the foundation. Nobody has heard of you yet.

Translate and embellish this setting without changing the template. The old PC is narrative-only: no compute, VRAM or occupied slot. Introduce the actual saved company and player, then render S3/S5.
